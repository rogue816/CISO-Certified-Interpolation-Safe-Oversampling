# PO-qG Experimental Protocol (Preregistered)

Version 1.0 — frozen before any full-benchmark execution.
Any deviation during the study must be logged in the Deviations section
with a reason, and reported in the paper.

---

## 1. Research questions and preregistered decision rules

**RQ1 (Merit).** Does PO-qG outperform the baseline tiers on predictive
performance?
*Decision rule:* success = PO-qG achieves a better mean rank than every
Tier-2 method on the primary metric (PR-AUC) for at least 3 of 4
classifiers, with Holm-adjusted p < 0.05 vs SMOTE. Failure to beat Tier-1
(class weights / random oversampling) on any classifier is reported
prominently, not buried.

**RQ2 (Mechanism).** Does each phase earn its keep?
*Decision rule:* for each ablation pair (full vs phase-off), Wilcoxon
signed-rank across evaluation datasets on PR-AUC. A phase is "confirmed"
if p < 0.05 in the expected direction; "not confirmed" is reported as-is
and the corresponding paper claims are weakened accordingly.

**RQ3 (When).** Does the certificate predict where the method helps?
*Decision rule:* Spearman correlation between per-dataset certified
fraction and the per-dataset performance delta (PO-qG minus SMOTE,
PR-AUC). Preregistered hypothesis: negative correlation (advantage
concentrates in low-certification / high-overlap datasets).

**RQ4 (Sensitivity).** Are alpha, beta, q live and interpretable?
Deliverables: alpha-ECDF ordering check per dataset; performance vs each
parameter with others fixed at tuned values; empirical comonotonicity
(Spearman rho of s_i vs gamma_i per dataset, reported as distribution).

**RQ5 (Cost).** Runtime vs baselines, split into fit (precompute) and
generate (per-sample) stages.

## 3. Evaluation design

- **Splits:** stratified 5-fold cross-validation, repeated 3 times
  (15 evaluations per dataset). n_splits is reduced to the minority count
  when the minority has fewer than 5 instances; datasets with minority
  < 2 in any training fold are excluded and listed.
- **Pairing:** the fold indices are generated once per dataset
  (seed = 42 + repeat index) and shared by ALL methods and classifiers.
- **Sampler stochasticity:** each stochastic sampler runs with
  seed = 1000 * repeat + fold, identical across methods.
- **Scores are aggregated as:** mean over the 15 fold-evaluations, per
  (dataset, classifier, method, metric). All statistics operate on these
  dataset-level means.

## 4. Methods under comparison

**Tier 1 — nulls (must-beat):**
1. None (no resampling)
2. ClassWeights (classifier trained with class_weight='balanced';
   for HGB via sample_weight)
3. RandomOverSampler

**Tier 2 — traditional interpolation family:**
4. SMOTE (k=5)
5. Borderline-SMOTE (borderline-2)
6. ADASYN
7. SMOTE-ENN
8. SMOTE-Tomek
9. KMeans-SMOTE (imblearn implementation; on failure see Section 7)

**Tier 3 — density/weight-aware relatives:**
10. Safe-Level-SMOTE (smote_variants)
11. MWMOTE (smote_variants)
12. G-SMOTE (smote_variants; optional, include if runtime permits)

**Tier 4 — generative (supplementary only):**
13. One SDV-based tabular oversampler on the scale suite + the 10 largest
    KEEL datasets. Reported in supplement with wall-clock costs; not part
    of RQ1 statistics.

**Proposed method and ablation ladder (RQ2):**
14. PO-qG (full, tuned configuration)
15. PO-qG[alpha=0]        — Phase I off (uniform anchors)
16. PO-qG[uniform-lambda] — Phase III off (lambda ~ U[0,1])
17. PO-qG[s=gamma]        — exact-certificate score variant
18. PO-qG[no-clearance-in-II] — Phase II weight = kernel only
    (isolates the locality kernel from neighbor safety)

Baseline hyperparameters are library defaults with k-type parameters set
to 5, matching PO-qG's k for fairness; no per-dataset tuning for any
method (global-configuration regime, applied identically to all).

## 5. Classifiers

Per-classifier analysis; no pooling across classifiers.
1. LogisticRegression (max_iter=2000)
2. SVC (RBF, probability via decision_function -> AUC/PR-AUC; G-mean at
   default threshold)
3. RandomForest (n_estimators=200)
4. HistGradientBoostingClassifier (defaults)
All with random_state = 42; refit from scratch per fold.

## 6. Metrics

**Primary:** PR-AUC (average precision).
**Secondary:** ROC-AUC, G-mean (at threshold 0.5 on predicted
probability; threshold policy fixed here and never re-optimized).
**Diagnostics (mechanism, not merit):**
- certified fraction |{gamma_i > 0}| / N1 per dataset (from fit)
- novelty: mean over synthetic points of distance to nearest ORIGINAL
  minority point (random oversampling scores ~0)
- spread: mean over synthetic points of distance to nearest OTHER
  synthetic point (duplication scores ~0)
- alpha-ECDF ordering: fraction of datasets where the three alpha-regime
  ECDFs of distance-to-majority are correctly ordered (stochastic
  dominance check at deciles)
- comonotonicity: Spearman rho(s_i, gamma_i) per dataset

## 7. Failure handling

If a sampler raises on a fold: record the failure, fall back to
None-resampling for that fold ONLY for pipeline continuity, and flag the
(dataset, method) cell. Any method failing on > 20% of folds of a dataset
is marked "failed" for that dataset; paired tests involving that method
drop that dataset (listed in appendix). Failures are reported in a
dedicated table — silent fallbacks are prohibited.

## 8. Hyperparameter tuning (PO-qG only)

- **Grid:** k in {5, 7}; alpha in {-1, 0, 0.5, 1, 2};
  q in {1.2, 1.5, 2.0}; beta in {0.1, 0.2, 0.4}; eps0 = 0.1 fixed.
  (90 configurations.)
- **Procedure:** full CV protocol (Section 3) on the 5 tuning datasets
  only. Selection criterion: highest mean over {tuning datasets} of the
  mean over {4 classifiers} of PR-AUC. Ties broken by G-mean, then by
  smaller |alpha| (parsimony).
- **Lock:** the selected configuration is written into this document
  (Section 10) before the evaluation suite is touched. The evaluation
  suite is run exactly once with it.
- The ablation ladder inherits the tuned values, changing only the
  ablated component.

## 9. Statistical analysis

- Per (classifier, metric): Friedman test over methods x evaluation
  datasets on dataset-level means. If p < 0.05: post-hoc comparisons of
  PO-qG (control) vs each other method with Holm correction
  (control-vs-all, not all-pairs).
- CD-style diagram with connecting bars drawn from the Holm results.
- Effect sizes: mean-rank differences and win/tie/loss counts
  (tie = |delta| < 0.001) reported alongside p-values.
- RQ2 ablations: paired Wilcoxon signed-rank, full vs each ablation,
  across evaluation datasets, per classifier; Holm across the 4 ablation
  comparisons.
- RQ3: Spearman correlation with 95% bootstrap CI (10,000 resamples).
- No additional tests beyond those listed here will be run; exploratory
  analyses, if any, are labeled as such.

## 13. RQ6 addendum (Distributional Fidelity) -- FROZEN 2026-07-09

Preregistered BEFORE any fidelity number is computed on the evaluation
suite. Machinery validation is permitted on the 5 tuning datasets only.

**Hypothesis.** Synthetic minority samples generated by PO-qG (locked
Section-10 configuration) are distributionally closer to HELD-OUT
minority data than those of SMOTE, Borderline-SMOTE, ADASYN, and ROS.

**Design.** The same paired 5x3 folds as the evaluation run. Per fold:
each sampler is fit on the standardized training fold and generates its
synthetic minority block; fidelity is measured against the TEST fold's
minority instances (never seen by the sampler). Held-out comparison
breaks the circularity of train-set geometric metrics: memorization
cannot cover held-out structure.

**Primary metric.** Unbiased squared Maximum Mean Discrepancy (MMD^2),
RBF kernel, bandwidth = median pairwise distance among the training
minority (computed once per fold, shared by all methods). Per-dataset
score = mean over usable folds. Lower is better. ROS is included as the
empirical-distribution reference floor.

**Secondary metric.** Orientation preservation: the largest principal
angle between the top-2 principal components of the synthetic block and
of the held-out minority (requires >= 3 held-out minority and d >= 2).
Lower is better.

**Diagnostics (reported, not merit).** Classifier two-sample test AUC
(logistic, 5-fold), explicitly caveated as duplication-favoring; the
existing novelty statistic accompanies it.

**Exclusions (recorded).** Folds with < 3 held-out minority instances
are excluded; datasets with < 8 usable folds are excluded from RQ6.
Sampler failures follow Section 7.

**Decision rule.** RQ6 is SUPPORTED if, on per-dataset MMD^2 across the
evaluation suite, PO-qG is significantly lower than at least 3 of the 4
baselines (paired Wilcoxon, Holm over the 4 comparisons, p < 0.05) and
not significantly higher than any. Any other outcome is NOT SUPPORTED
and is reported as such. No re-tuning in response to RQ6 results.
