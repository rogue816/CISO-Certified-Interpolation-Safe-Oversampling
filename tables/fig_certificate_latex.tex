  \caption{The clearance certificate across four controlled minority
  geometries. Majority instances are grey; minority instances are
  coloured by safety score $s_i$; anchors with a positive certificate
  ($\gamma_i>0$, Lemma~\ref{lem:margin}) are ringed in red. The certified
  fraction (panel titles) responds to structure: it is complete for
  well-separated clusters (a), collapses under overlap (b), and---because
  the certificate is \emph{per anchor}---distinguishes a safe cluster from
  bridging small disjuncts within the same dataset (c) and tracks a
  curved manifold (d). Axes are the standardized features.}
  \label{fig:certificate}
